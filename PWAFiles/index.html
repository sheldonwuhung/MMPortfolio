Introduction

What is Badminton?

In badminton, two opponents or groups hit a shuttlecock/birdie over a net with with rackets, scoring points when one party fails to return it. It is a fun activity played by many people around the world and you can learn how to play as well! In this app, you will get to know how to grip a racket, the dimensions of a court, and the rules of a game!

Two Main Grips

Forehand

Hold the racket straight where your index and thumb form a "V" shape.

This will be used to receive dominant sided shots

Backhand

Hold the racket straight where your thumb is leaning directly on the side of the racket.

This will be used to receive non-dominant sided shots.

Game Rules

In badminton singles, the server must stand inside the service court and hit the shuttle diagonally to the opponent’s service court. The serve must be delivered underarm with the shuttle below waist height, and the server scores only when serving. After each rally, players switch sides only when the server wins a point. In doubles, serving is also diagonal, underarm, and below the waist, but the serving team changes court positions after winning a rally when serving. The receiving team stays in position until it wins the rally, at which point the new server serves from the appropriate side. Games are played to 21 points, win by 2, with a maximum of 30 points, and a rally is lost if the shuttle hits the net, lands out of bounds, or is struck more than once by the same side.
